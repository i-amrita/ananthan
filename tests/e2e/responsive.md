# Flow: responsive layout

## Goal
The site is read on a phone first. At 375px nothing may overflow horizontally, the nav must stay reachable, the hero must still scroll sideways and the lightbox must fit the viewport.

## Checks
- `/`, `/research`, `/life`, `/photography` do not scroll horizontally at 375px.
- The nav and the research link stay visible.
- The hero track keeps a horizontal overflow mode.
- The lightbox image fits within 375px.
- The research list stays within the viewport width.
